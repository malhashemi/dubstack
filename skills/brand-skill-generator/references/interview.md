# The Brand Interview

The interview is how the brand is developed with the user: rounds on one page, each showing the current understanding of the brand and every question ready to ask, until the identity is ready to codify. It serves the kit. Once the brand skill exists, the interview is removed.

## Where it lives

The interview folder is `<work-dir>` throughout this skill.

- **When the project has a Dossier tree,** as its `AGENTS.md` entry records, use `brand-interview/` at the root of the tree's local copy. Follow the entry's synchronization instructions before writing, and publish with its commands, so answers and comments persist on Dossier.
- **Otherwise,** use a new folder in the operating system's temporary directory and open the page locally. The browser keeps answers until the user hands them back.

```text
<work-dir>/
  working.md     the record
  index.html     the interview page
  candidates/    provisional palettes, marks, and other materials
```

Resume the same folder when the work continues, and give its path when pausing. Keep it out of the final kit. Recreatable renders and intermediate exports can go to system temporary storage, but what is needed to resume belongs in the folder.

## The record

`working.md` is the agent's record and the content source for the page. Keep it current rather than cumulative:

- **Understanding:** each established item with its basis: source material, the user's decision, or delegated judgment.
- **Open:** each question ready to ask, with a stable ID, its options and their consequences, and the recommendation with its reason.
- **Waiting:** questions that depend on another answer, with what they wait on.
- **Coverage:** every foundation and every dimension of [Verbal identity](verbal-identity-checklist.md) and [Visual foundations](visual-foundations-checklist.md), marked established, not applicable with a reason, deferred, or open.

IDs and option letters keep their meaning for the life of the interview. When a decision changes what a question or option means, give it a new ID or letter, and ask again rather than applying an earlier answer to it.

Distinguish established facts and constraints, interpretations to verify, proposed directions, accepted decisions, and explicit deferrals. An agent-selected decision made under delegated authority identifies that delegation and its limits. Record what the user's feedback applies to: accepting a concept does not automatically accept every font, phrase, and color shown with it. A scope limitation remains in force through later selections unless the user changes it. For example, preferring a dark presentation does not establish a policy against light-mode support.

A choice is consequential when plausible alternatives would change the audience priority, promise, desired perception, or meaningful creative constraints on future work. Implementation details realize established direction within those constraints. Calling an imagery restriction, messaging policy, or motion character a default does not make it an implementation detail; establish its basis and demonstrate its effect before treating it as a brand rule.

## Phases

1. **Foundations.** The offering, priority audience, positioning and promise, personality and desired perception, intended applications and their priority, the name, assets to preserve, and the creative authority the user delegates, drawn from [Brand context](brand-context.md). Settle what the evidence establishes and show it for correction. Each foundation rests on authoritative source material, a user decision, or judgment within the user's delegation, and expression opens once the user has confirmed the foundations as a whole: a palette chosen before anyone has said who the brand is for is the failure this prevents. When authoritative material establishes them, that confirmation can come in the first round.
2. **Expression.** Verbal and visual identity together, until both checklists are accounted for. Every expression round carries the open verbal questions beside the visual ones and shows choices in applications where language and visuals work together, so a selection among visual treatments never stands in for the rest of the identity.
3. **Applications.** The system applied to the intended uses at real sizes and conditions. Gaps it exposes return to expression as questions.

## Rounds

The user may answer hours later, so ask every ready question at once. Hold one back only when a hard choice deserves a round of its own, and record why it waits.

Fit each question to what it needs:

- A missing fact calls for a focused question, with a working assumption only when it is reasonable and labeled.
- A taste decision calls for concrete alternatives or a proposal, a recommendation, and visible consequences.
- A technical or production constraint calls for inspection or a feasibility check, not an aesthetic vote.
- A conflict calls for reconciling the sources and intent, not silently selecting the most frequent value.

Answer from the evidence before asking, and ask only what would change a decision or enable an intended application. The checklists define coverage, not questions to put to the user: a dimension the evidence settles is shown as understanding, not asked.

The page shows the current understanding, open to correction, and the round's questions. The user can choose, reject every option, propose another direction, and comment on anything settled, and returns the response through a copyable handoff. On Dossier, answers and comments also persist in the tree. Read [Visual review](visual-review.md) before briefing the page.

Fold each response into the record as [Visual review](visual-review.md#turn-feedback-into-decisions) describes. Then promote the questions that response opens, and revise the page for the next round.

## Ending the interview

The interview ends when the coverage is accounted for and the identity is [ready to codify](../SKILL.md#establish-readiness-to-codify). After packaging, [finish and remove the interview folder](brand-kit-spec.md#finish-and-remove-the-interview-folder).
