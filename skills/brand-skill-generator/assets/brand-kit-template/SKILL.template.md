## Apply with judgment

Use the brand's intent and the reasons behind its choices to guide expression. Context can change emphasis, density, format, and detail while the underlying promise, voice, and recognizable qualities remain coherent.

Treat established constraints as contracts and use judgment within the documented flexible areas. Examples illustrate decisions; their incidental layouts, wording, and technology are not universal requirements. Use the recorded reasons to interpret unfamiliar cases. When guidance leaves a consequential brand choice unresolved, distinguish the proposed interpretation from established identity.

Use canonical assets, documented values and roles, and supported claims. Account for the actual language, accessibility needs, and production conditions when interpreting the identity. Distinguish facts, accepted decisions, working assumptions, and unselected concepts. The user's current explicit direction governs conflicts; identify consequential changes it makes to the identity.

An implementation detail, a repeated default, or an unselected concept does not become a brand rule merely through use. Carry forward established decisions and their authority when interpreting or extending the system.

## Assess brand consistency

Judge expression against the relevant identity: its promise and supported claims, voice, hierarchy, relationships among visual elements, asset use, and governing constraints. Matching individual values alone does not establish a coherent expression of the brand.

Distinguish permitted variation from a departure that changes the identity. Make deliberate deviations, provisional interpretations, and unsupported assumptions explicit. An example demonstrates one application; it does not establish a universal layout or coverage of every context.

## Evolve through use

Capture a reusable pattern, missing rule, or correction when it fits the established identity, has proved useful in its actual context, and offers value beyond a one-off artifact. Reuse or refine an existing resource first. A promising unverified idea remains a proposal.

Preserve the useful brand knowledge as guidance with reasons, a canonical asset, or an example with its relevant states and variations. Register its location, purpose, use conditions, and maturity. Keep essential guidance and examples within the kit. A link to a maintained implementation can provide additional context without making that project necessary to understand the brand.

Keep updates within the authority of the current task. Extending an established pattern differs from changing the brand's promise, voice, palette, or typography. Record a proposed identity change separately until it has the necessary authority; ordinary application does not itself establish that authority.

Keep tokens authoritative for values and guidelines authoritative for intent and usage. Preserve approved palette values and print references until an identity change establishes replacements. Update affected portable values, assets, examples, and resource links together. Keep the human overview aligned with the identity and account for effects on existing consumers.

When adding guidance, explain the intended expression, constraints, and reasons that improve future judgment. State exact brand rules where consistency depends on them, while keeping examples and defaults distinguishable from requirements. Put common essentials in this entrypoint, detailed knowledge in relevant resources, and clear reading conditions beside their links. Preserve the shared application, brand-consistency, and evolution blocks; add brand-specific guidance around them. Remove stale or redundant guidance while preserving useful detail. Record consequential changes, their basis, and any remaining gaps in the identity.
